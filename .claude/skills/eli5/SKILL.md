# eli5

Explain like I'm a curious 15-year-old who has never studied this topic, using an HTML artifact with big pictures and short text.

The topic is whatever the user asked to have explained in their message.

Pitch it at a high-school level:

- Assume no prior knowledge of the topic, but normal teenage general knowledge (school science and math, everyday tech, the internet).
- Real terms are welcome once each is introduced with a plain-language definition. Do not talk down or use baby analogies.
- One idea per picture. Prefer a concrete running example carried through every step over abstract diagrams.
- Keep text short, but a sentence or two per picture is fine. Add a one-line "why it matters" or "how it's used in real life" where it helps.
- End with a short recap that a reader could repeat back to a friend.
